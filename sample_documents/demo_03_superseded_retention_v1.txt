TruthGuard Compliance and Data Retention Standard
Title: Compliance and Data Retention Standard
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2024-01-15
Authority: OFFICIAL
Source Type: POLICY

1. Customer Audit Logs: Customer audit logs must be retained for exactly 365 days (1 year) from generation date before purging.
2. Backup Archive: Snapshots are preserved in cool storage for the duration of the 365-day retention window.
