TruthGuard Production Operations Runbook
Title: Production Operations Runbook
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Authority: OFFICIAL
Source Type: POLICY

1. Engineer Workstations: Production session idle timeout is set to exactly 60 minutes for all engineer terminals to prevent premature disconnections during system maintenance tasks.
2. Maintenance Access: On-call engineers may maintain active command-line sessions without re-authenticating for up to one full hour.
