1. Building Hours: The main headquarters building is accessible to badged personnel Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
2. Cafeteria Services: Lunch services operate between 11:30 AM and 2:00 PM daily.
3. Visitor Badges: All external visitors must surrender temporary visitor badges at the security reception desk upon departure.
