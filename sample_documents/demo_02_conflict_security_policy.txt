TruthGuard Access Control Security Policy
Title: Access Control Security Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Authority: OFFICIAL
Source Type: POLICY

1. Terminal Timeout: Production session idle timeout must be strictly enforced at 15 minutes for all engineer terminals without exception.
2. Re-authentication: Idle terminal connections exceeding 15 minutes must be severed by bastion proxies to protect sensitive infrastructure.
