TruthGuard Engineering Baseline Standard
Title: Engineering Baseline Standard
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Authority: OFFICIAL
Source Type: SPECIFICATION

1. Encryption at Rest: All production databases must enforce AES-256-GCM encryption for persistent volumes.
2. Transport Security: All production database client connections must enforce TLS 1.3 encryption with certificate validation. Unencrypted database traffic is blocked by firewall rules.
