TruthGuard Compliance and Data Retention Standard
Title: Compliance and Data Retention Standard
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 2025-06-01
Authority: EXECUTIVE
Supersedes: Version 1.0
Source Type: POLICY

1. Customer Audit Logs: Customer audit logs must be retained for 730 days (2 full years) to satisfy expanded SOC2 and ISO27001 compliance standards.
2. Supersession Notice: This policy formally revokes and supersedes the older Version 1.0 365-day retention requirement.
